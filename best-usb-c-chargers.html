How to Choose a USB-C Charger

SmartPick guide · Updated November 2026

Start with wattage, then check USB-C Power Delivery, port count, cable quality and safety certifications. A 20–30W charger is often enough for many phones; tablets may benefit from 30–45W, and some laptops need 65W or more. Check your device requirements rather than assuming more watts always means faster charging.

Quick checklist

  • Compare the features that matter for your specific use.
  • Check dimensions, compatibility and return policies.
  • Read recent reviews, paying attention to repeated complaints rather than single anecdotes.
  • Compare total price, warranty and replacement parts.
