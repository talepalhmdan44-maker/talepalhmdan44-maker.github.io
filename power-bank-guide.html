How to Choose a Power Bank

SmartPick guide · Updated November 2026

Capacity is commonly advertised in mAh, but actual energy delivered depends on voltage conversion and losses. Compare USB-C output wattage, cable compatibility, number of ports, weight and built-in safety features. For air travel, check airline rules for lithium batteries and keep power banks in carry-on baggage.

Quick checklist

  • Compare the features that matter for your specific use.
  • Check dimensions, compatibility and return policies.
  • Read recent reviews, paying attention to repeated complaints rather than single anecdotes.
  • Compare total price, warranty and replacement parts.
