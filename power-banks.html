Power Banks: Capacity, Output and Travel

mAh figures are usually quoted at the internal cell voltage, so usable energy delivered to a phone is lower after conversion losses.

Updated November 19, 2026 · Research-based buyer guide; not a hands-on product test.

What to consider

mAh figures are usually quoted at the internal cell voltage, so usable energy delivered to a phone is lower after conversion losses. Check USB-C output wattage and supported charging protocols; a phone-focused bank may not be suitable for a laptop. Compare port count, shared output, the time needed to recharge the bank and its size and weight. Before flying, verify current airline rules for spare lithium batteries and carry-on limits.

SmartPick takeaway

Buy for the devices you actually carry; check watt-hours and airline rules before travel.

Confirm current specifications, compatibility, availability, seller details, warranty and return terms on the retailer’s product page. SmartPick has not physically tested the products discussed here.
