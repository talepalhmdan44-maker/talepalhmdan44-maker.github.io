Desk Lamps: Brightness, Color and Glare

Look for an adjustable arm or head so light can be directed onto the work area.

Updated November 19, 2026 · Research-based buyer guide; not a hands-on product test.

What to consider

Look for an adjustable arm or head so light can be directed onto the work area. Brightness is often expressed in lumens and color temperature in kelvin; adjustable settings can help across different tasks. Position the lamp to avoid screen reflections and distracting shadows. Check the base stability, power connection, controls and whether the LED or power supply can be replaced.

SmartPick takeaway

Adjustability, glare control and comfortable light are the most useful features.

Confirm current specifications, compatibility, availability, seller details, warranty and return terms on the retailer’s product page. SmartPick has not physically tested the products discussed here.
