Robot Vacuums: Mapping, Floors and Maintenance

Consider your floor plan, thresholds, rugs, cables and clutter.

Updated November 19, 2026 · Research-based buyer guide; not a hands-on product test.

What to consider

Consider your floor plan, thresholds, rugs, cables and clutter. Mapping can help with room-by-room cleaning, but check how the robot handles obstacles in your actual home. Compare brush design if you have long hair or pets. Mopping attachments vary and should not be assumed to deep-clean floors. Self-emptying docks need space and may require replacement bags. Check availability and cost of filters, brushes and other parts.

SmartPick takeaway

A model that fits your layout and is easy to maintain can beat a feature-heavy model that struggles at home.

Confirm current specifications, compatibility, availability, seller details, warranty and return terms on the retailer’s product page. SmartPick has not physically tested the products discussed here.
