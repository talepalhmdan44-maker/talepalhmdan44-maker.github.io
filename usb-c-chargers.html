USB-C Chargers: Wattage, Ports and Compatibility

Count the devices you charge and check each device maker’s recommended input.

Updated November 19, 2026 · Research-based buyer guide; not a hands-on product test.

What to consider

Count the devices you charge and check each device maker’s recommended input. USB-C describes the connector, not a guaranteed charging speed. Look for USB Power Delivery support and check whether PPS is needed for your device’s fastest charging. On multi-port chargers, total output is shared between connected devices. Use a cable rated for the power you need and choose a reputable maker with clear electrical ratings.

SmartPick takeaway

Choose a reputable USB-PD charger with enough output for your main device, plus a properly rated cable.

Confirm current specifications, compatibility, availability, seller details, warranty and return terms on the retailer’s product page. SmartPick has not physically tested the products discussed here.
